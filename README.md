# Special Checkers

Special Checkers is a Python game made with Processing.
It mixes classic Checkers and Rock Paper Scissors.
You play against the computer.

## Installation and Execution

### 1. Navigate to the project

Open your terminal and move into the project folder:

```bash
cd path/to/Special-checkers
```

### 2. Configure Processing

The game needs the Python Mode in Processing.
Open the Processing application.
Click on the mode menu in the top right corner.
Choose "Add Mode", search for "Python Mode" and click install.

### 3. Launch the game

Open the file `projet_nsi.py` in Processing. Click the Play button at the top left to start the game.

---

## How to Play

### 1. Select the difficulty

On the main menu, press the key for the level you want:

* Press E for Easy
* Press M for Medium
* Press H for Hard

### 2. Win the Rock Paper Scissors round

At the start of each turn, you play against the computer to get the right to move:

* Press 1 for Rock
* Press 2 for Paper
* Press 3 for Scissors

If you win, it is your turn. If the computer wins, it will move automatically.

### 3. Move your pieces

Click on a brown piece with your mouse to select it. Then, use your keyboard to move:

* Press A to move Forward-Left
* Press E to move Forward-Right
* Press Q to move Backward-Left (Queens only)
* Press D to move Backward-Right (Queens only)

### 4. Game Rules

To capture a computer piece, you need to jump over it.
When your piece reaches the top row, it becomes a Queen. Queens have a gold center and can move backward.
The game ends when one player loses all their pieces.
Press R to restart or click the red STOP button to quit.
